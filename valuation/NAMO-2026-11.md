## The gate math

₹1 cr/month platform revenue at ₹450 take per ₹1,499 consult = **~740 paid consults a day** [tested arithmetic].
Astrotalk does ~₹150 cr/month. The gate is 0.7% of the leader — reachable only with paid acquisition.

## Hidden assumption

The pitch treats **NAMO as the asset**. The evidence in the deck (catalogue, ads, SEO agents) is for **Ananta AI Labs**,
not for NAMO. Option that breaks it: value Labs as an internal agency first (cost saved across group brands, priced at what
the agencies it replaces charge) and fund NAMO from that line — NAMO then becomes a call option, not the thesis.

## Contradiction to fix in the deck

"Pre-revenue" sits beside a PRD with seven live paid lines and a live gateway. Either state the real revenue to date
(even ₹0 from N paid attempts) or say "monetisation live, revenue not yet material". An investor who opens the app will see prices.
